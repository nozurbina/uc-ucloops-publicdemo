# Source: Consumer Social Media Mentions — BorderBlend
**Source type:** Social listening
**Period:** 90 days (Q3)
**Platforms:** Instagram, TikTok
**Total mentions sampled:** 25 representative mentions from a larger sample of approximately 840 tagged or identified mentions
**Selection methodology:** Sample selected to represent the range of sentiment, topic, and format. Mentions are paraphrased or lightly edited to remove identifying usernames; verbatim quotes are preserved where they represent the language of interest. Sentiment tagged: Positive (P), Negative (N), Mixed (M), Neutral/Informational (I).
