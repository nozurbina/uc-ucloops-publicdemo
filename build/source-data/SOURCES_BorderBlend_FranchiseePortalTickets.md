# Source: Franchisee Portal Support Request Summaries
**Source type:** Support tickets
**Period:** Rolling 6 months
**Channel:** Franchisee portal support request form and support phone line
**Total tickets:** 15 anonymised summaries drawn from a larger sample of 94 tickets in the period
**Selection methodology:** Representative sample across primary issue categories. Operator names and identifying details removed.

---

## Ticket summaries

---

### FT #001
**Category:** Marketing materials — localisation
**Market:** Quebec
**Summary:** Franchisee requests French-language versions of the current seasonal campaign assets. Reports that the English-language assets have been live for three weeks but French equivalents have not been provided. Has been adapting English materials independently but notes the process is time-consuming and results in inconsistency with the national campaign.
**Resolution:** Brand team confirmed French assets are in development; estimated delivery in two weeks. Franchisee expressed frustration at the delay, noting this is a recurring pattern with every campaign.
**Signal:** French localisation timeline gap — consistently late relative to English campaign release. Consistent with Marc Bélanger interview (BB-INT002). Pattern noted: "recurring."

---

### FT #002
**Category:** Marketing materials — digital asset access
**Market:** Toronto
**Summary:** Franchisee cannot locate the current version of the logo file in high resolution for use in a local event listing. Downloaded a version from the portal but it was low-resolution. Spent time searching without finding the correct file.
**Resolution:** Agent located the correct file in a subfolder and provided a direct link. Franchisee noted they could not have found it independently.
**Signal:** Asset organisation and discoverability problem in the portal. Consistent with Yuki Tanaka's description (BB-INT004) of the portal being organised by document type rather than user need.

---

### FT #003
**Category:** Operations — ingredient substitution
**Market:** Calgary
**Summary:** Franchisee reports a supply disruption affecting one of the core menu ingredients (fresh coriander). Asks whether a substitution is brand-compliant, and if so, what the approved substitution is.
**Resolution:** Brand team confirmed approved substitution and advised franchisee to note the change on their menu board with suggested language.
**Signal:** Supply chain disruption is an operational scenario the portal doesn't pre-empt with guidance. Real-time decision support for substitutions is not documented upfront.

---

### FT #004
**Category:** Brand compliance — social media
**Market:** Vancouver
**Summary:** HQ flags a franchisee's recent Instagram post that uses unapproved copy. The post includes a claim ("the most authentic Mexican food in Vancouver") that violates the brand guidelines (no superlative claims, no "authentic" language in connection with a fusion brand). Franchisee appears unaware of the restriction.
**Resolution:** Franchisee was contacted, acknowledged the issue, and removed the post. No sanction applied. Remedial training on brand voice guidelines recommended.
**Signal:** Brand guidelines are not being consistently applied by franchisees. "Authentic" and superlative restrictions may not be prominent enough in onboarding. The issue was caught after posting — reactive rather than preventive.

---

### FT #005
**Category:** Marketing materials — campaign economics
**Market:** Toronto (multiple)
**Summary:** Two Toronto franchisees contact support separately with concerns about the pricing guidance in the seasonal campaign promotional kit. Both state that the suggested discount pricing in the kit produces a margin that doesn't cover their operating costs at Toronto labour rates.
**Resolution:** Escalated to the brand marketing team. No immediate resolution provided; franchisees advised that campaign economics feedback was noted for future campaign planning.
**Signal:** Campaign pricing assumes cost structures that do not apply in high-cost-of-living markets. Consistent with Diego Montoya (BB-INT001) on the Fuego Nights campaign. Two independent tickets on the same issue in the same market during the same campaign.

---

### FT #006
**Category:** Onboarding — materials access
**Market:** BC
**Summary:** New franchisee (first month of operation) reports that several of the onboarding modules in the portal are showing as unavailable or "coming soon." Asks when the content will be accessible.
**Resolution:** Agent investigated and confirmed that three onboarding modules had not been published following a portal migration. Modules restored within 24 hours.
**Signal:** Onboarding content gaps due to a technical migration error — affecting a new franchisee in their most critical period. The franchisee would not have known what they were missing.

---

### FT #007
**Category:** Operations — staff training materials
**Market:** Edmonton
**Summary:** Franchisee asks if there are training materials for new staff covering food safety, customer service, and preparation standards. Has recently hired two new crew members and needs structured onboarding for them.
**Resolution:** Agent provided links to the staff training section of the portal. Franchisee noted they were unaware these existed.
**Signal:** Staff-level training materials exist but are not prominently communicated to franchisees as part of the onboarding process. Consistent with the general discoverability problem in the portal.

---

### FT #008
**Category:** Marketing materials — brand compliance, positive
**Market:** Ontario
**Summary:** Franchisee submits a self-produced promotional graphic for optional review before posting. Wants to confirm it is brand-compliant.
**Resolution:** Reviewed by brand team; minor colour adjustment requested; franchisee made the change and posted.
**Signal:** A franchisee voluntarily seeking pre-publication brand compliance review is notable — this is good practice. The support for it exists (the review process) but is not formalized as a recommended step for all franchisees.

---

### FT #009
**Category:** Technical — POS system
**Market:** BC
**Summary:** Franchisee reports that the POS system integration with the consumer app ordering flow has been producing discrepancies. Orders placed via the app are not always appearing on the POS terminal in time, resulting in customers arriving for pickup and finding their order not started.
**Resolution:** Technical issue escalated. Franchisee advised to take manual orders from app customers until resolved. Resolution took four days.
**Signal:** App ordering and in-store POS integration failure creates a frontline customer experience problem. Consistent with Priya Sharma (BB-INT008) who noted "wait time was wrong" on the app. Suggests the order flow problem is systemic, not isolated.

### FT #010
**Category:** Marketing materials — French content
**Market:** Quebec
**Summary:** Franchisee notes that the French version of the new menu item description contains a translation error — the word used for "pickled" is incorrect in Quebec French (European French term used). Asks for a correction.
**Resolution:** Error confirmed. Corrected version published within 48 hours. Franchisee's feedback credited for catching the issue.
**Signal:** Translation quality in French-language materials is inconsistent — European French terms used in Quebec context. Consistent with Patrick Leblanc (VER-INT009) and Marc Bélanger (BB-INT002) on the Quebec French dialect specifically.

---

### FT #011
**Category:** Franchise agreement — territory question
**Market:** Ontario
**Summary:** Franchisee asks whether they can operate their truck at a location that falls at the edge of their designated territory, in response to an event invitation from a community organiser.
**Resolution:** Franchise operations team reviewed the territory mapping. Confirmed the location is within territory. Franchisee informed.
**Signal:** Territory ambiguity at edges is a recurring question type. Suggests franchise territory boundaries are not always self-evident to franchisees.

---

### FT #012
**Category:** Marketing materials — seasonal kit delay
**Market:** Multiple (4 tickets combined)
**Summary:** Four franchisees in different cities contact support in the same week noting that the seasonal autumn campaign kit has not yet been provided with two weeks remaining before the launch date.
**Resolution:** Brand team acknowledged a production delay. Kit was delivered one week before launch.
**Signal:** Campaign kit delivery timelines are too compressed. One week before launch is insufficient for franchisees to adapt, translate where needed, and execute at the local level. Four-city simultaneous complaint is a multi-source signal.

---

### FT #013
**Category:** Operations — health inspection
**Market:** Toronto
**Summary:** Franchisee is due for a municipal health inspection and asks whether there is documentation they should have on file for the inspector regarding ingredient sourcing and food handling certifications.
**Resolution:** Relevant documentation provided. Franchisee noted they had not received this documentation in onboarding.
**Signal:** Regulatory compliance documentation is not reliably included in the onboarding package. Could create exposure for franchisees in markets with active inspection regimes.

---

### FT #014
**Category:** Support — emotional
**Market:** BC
**Summary:** New franchisee (four months in) contacts support describing significant stress and uncertainty. Revenue has been lower than anticipated; they are concerned about whether their business is viable. Asks if there is anyone to talk to about the business performance expectations.
**Resolution:** Agent escalated to a business development contact. Follow-up call scheduled. No financial benchmarking data was available to share.
**Signal:** New franchisee experiencing financial anxiety with no access to benchmarking data to calibrate expectations. Consistent with Yuki Tanaka (BB-INT004) and Kenji Watanabe (BB-INT005) on the benchmarking gap. The emotional dimension — asking "is there anyone to talk to" — suggests the support need is not purely informational.

---

### FT #015
**Category:** Operations — weather contingency
**Market:** Prairie markets (multiple)
**Summary:** Two Alberta franchisees ask whether there is guidance on weather contingency planning for winter operations. Notes that the current operations guide does not cover cold-weather truck management.
**Resolution:** No dedicated cold-weather operations guide exists. Franchisees were pointed to general operations resources and advised to connect with other Prairie-market franchisees via Slack.
**Signal:** Regional operational gaps in brand documentation. Prairie/cold-weather operations is a real and recurring operational context not covered in the general guide. Advising franchisees to use Slack as the resolution acknowledges the peer network as a default support channel.
